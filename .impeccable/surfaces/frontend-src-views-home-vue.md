## Direction contract

### THESIS
The Wabi-Sabi Paper Folio & Atelier: Replaces cold blueprint lines, mechanical crosshairs, and clinical coordinates with the warmth of unbleached washi paper, natural linen weave, raw terracotta, and quiet Scandinavian timber tones. Treats code, art, and writing as hand-hewn human crafts rather than technical outputs.

### OWN-WORLD

Generous negative space (Ma), delicate organic rounded corners, tactile paper-grain noise overlays, and gentle watercolor/fluid ambient movement.

### STORY
The visitor enters a quiet, sunlit study. They immediately perceive Jason as a thoughtful writer, artist, and coder whose relationship with technology is organic, deliberate, and deeply human. They browse curated projects presented as tactile artifact folios with breathing room.
